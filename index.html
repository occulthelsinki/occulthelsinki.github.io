Occult Helsinki provides professional guided walking tours showcasing the highlights and hidden gems of Finland’s capital. Perfect for all curious minds and lovers of art, architecture, and history.

All tours are operated by local Finnish performer Henri Tahvanainen, who personally guides guests and immerses them in cultural heritage while helping them to find hidden details in their surroundings.

Drawing from history, folklore, mythology, and symbolism, he serves guests meaningful experiences through engaging and insightful storytelling with a side of warm local hospitality.

As the cultural gateway to the North, Helsinki is the ideal stop before or after visiting Lapland, Rovaniemi, and the Arctic Circle.

Whether you are heading north or returning south, this walk provides the essential Finnish folklore and mythological context to enrich your entire journey through Finland.

Considered the best thing to do in Helsinki for discerning travelers who want an authentic perspective of the city beyond the mundane tourist traps.

Discover a side of the capital that most travelers—and even locals—never get to experience. Strictly limited spots available.

Tour Information
  • Route: Approximately 3–4 km (1.8–2.4 miles) through central Helsinki.
  • Pace: Comfortable and conversational.
  • Duration: 1.5 to 2 hours (90–120 minutes).
  • Capacity: Limited to 12 spots to preserve exclusivity.
