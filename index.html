The Experience & Guide

Occult Helsinki provides professional guided walking tours showcasing the highlights and hidden gems of Finland’s capital. Perfect for all curious minds and lovers of art, architecture, and history[cite: 1, 2].

All tours are operated by local Finnish performer Henri Tahvanainen, who personally guides guests and immerses them in cultural heritage while helping them discover hidden details in their surroundings[cite: 1, 2].

Drawing from history, folklore, mythology, and symbolism, he serves guests with meaningful experiences through engaging and insightful storytelling, with a side of warm local hospitality[cite: 1, 2].

Considered the best thing to do in Helsinki for discerning travelers who want an authentic perspective of the city beyond the mundane tourist traps[cite: 1, 2]. Strictly limited spots available[cite: 1, 2].
